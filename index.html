NDMAA Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Negros Digital Marketing & Advertising Agency (NDMAA) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our Facebook Page, messages, and inquiry or lead forms.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, business name, and information you voluntarily provide about your inquiry or service needs.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver requested services, and communicate about ongoing work. If you agree to receive promotional messages, you may ask us to stop at any time.

Advertising location

Our advertising campaigns target audiences in the Philippines, with other countries excluded through location targeting. This targeting does not guarantee that information is stored or processed exclusively in the Philippines.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with authorized team members and service providers when necessary to handle your inquiry or provide our services, or when required by law.

Facebook and other platforms process information under their own privacy policies and may process it outside the Philippines.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information and restrict access to authorized personnel. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, contractual requirements, or legal obligations. Information is then securely deleted or anonymized.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or request deletion or blocking. You may also raise a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, please message our official Facebook Page: Negros Digital Marketing & Advertising Agency (NDMAA).

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our practices change. The latest version will show its effective date.
